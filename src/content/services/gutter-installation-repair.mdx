Gutters do their most important work in Colorado during the weeks nobody thinks about them — when spring snowpack melts off the roof in an afternoon, or a summer cell drops an inch of rain in twenty minutes. Undersized or failing gutters dump that water next to your foundation, carve channels through landscaping, and feed the ice dams that wreck eaves in January.

## Seamless gutters, sized correctly

We fabricate seamless 5-inch and 6-inch K-style aluminum gutters on-site, cut to the exact length of each run. On steep or large roof planes — common on newer two-story homes in the metro — we upsize to 6-inch gutters with 3×4-inch downspouts, which move roughly 40% more water than builder-grade systems. Hidden hangers screw into the fascia every 24 inches so heavy, wet spring snow can't pull the run loose.

## Repairs and realignment

Sagging runs, separated end caps, and downspouts that discharge against the foundation are all fixable without full replacement. We re-pitch runs to drain fully (standing water is what splits seams in a freeze), reseal joints with tripolymer sealant rather than hardware-store silicone, and add extensions or splash blocks so water lands where it should.

## Gutter guards that work with hail

Not every guard survives Colorado. We install low-profile stainless micro-mesh guards that shed cottonwood fluff and pine needles but sit tight enough to the gutter that hail doesn't trampoline granules into the mesh. If you're replacing your roof with us, gutters and guards installed in the same project are priced at a package rate — the crew and equipment are already there.
