Commercial roofs in the Denver metro fail differently than residential ones: ponding water works on seams all summer, hail bruises single-ply membranes, and rooftop units get serviced by trades who leave punctures behind. Catching those problems early is the difference between a $900 repair and a six-figure tear-off.

## Systems we install

- **TPO** — the workhorse for flat and low-slope roofs. Heat-welded seams, bright white reflectivity that cuts cooling loads, and strong hail performance in 60- and 80-mil thicknesses.
- **EPDM** — proven rubber membrane for roofs where ballast or dark surfaces make sense; excellent in freeze-thaw.
- **Standing-seam metal** — for sloped commercial and mixed-use buildings that want a 40-year service life.
- **Re-cover systems** — where code and moisture scans allow, a new membrane over the existing assembly saves tear-off cost and landfill fees.

## Built around your operations

Tear-off noise over an occupied dental office at 10 AM is a failure of planning, not a necessity. We sequence commercial work in phases — early starts, weekend sections over sensitive tenants, staged material deliveries that don't block your parking — and the roof ties in watertight at the end of every shift, because afternoon storms here don't check the schedule.

## Maintenance keeps warranties alive

Manufacturer warranties on commercial systems require documented maintenance. Our program pairs semi-annual inspections with photo logs, drain clearing, and seam checks after major hail events, filed so that if you ever need the warranty, the paper trail is already built.
