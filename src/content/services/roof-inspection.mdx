A roof inspection is cheap insurance in a climate that throws hail, 90-mph chinook winds, and 60-degree temperature swings at your shingles. We inspect roofs for three main reasons: after storms (documented for potential insurance claims), before real-estate closings (for buyers, sellers, and agents on a deadline), and as routine checkups for roofs past their tenth year.

## What the 21 points cover

On the roof: shingle condition and seal, hail bruising, wind creasing, granule loss, exposed nails, ridge caps, valleys, step and counter flashing, chimney crowns, pipe boots, skylights, and satellite mounts. In the attic: decking stains, daylight at penetrations, insulation condition, and whether intake and exhaust ventilation are actually balanced — the item most inspections skip and the one that quietly voids shingle warranties. At the ground: gutters, downspouts, drip edge, and fascia.

## The report you actually get

Every finding is photographed and labeled, so you're never taking our word for something you can't see. The report separates items into three buckets: monitor (normal aging), plan (needs work in the next year or two), and act (fix now). For real-estate transactions we can turn the report around within 24 hours of access, and we're happy to walk the findings with the buyer's or seller's agent by phone.

## Why free?

Because it's how we earn replacement and repair work without pressure. Around a third of our inspections find nothing worth fixing — and telling a homeowner "your roof is fine, see you in two years" is the cheapest marketing we do.
